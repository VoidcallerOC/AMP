The Northline note

01/03

The best things in a home are the ones that quietly become part of its rhythm.

Objects with a point of view.

We believe everyday things deserve more attention. A cup that feels right in your hand. A shelf that makes room for the good stuff. A gift that says, “I know you.”

Northline started with a workbench, a borrowed kiln, and a hunch that local could still feel special. A decade later, we make small runs of objects, host patient hands-on workshops, and keep the kettle on for anyone curious enough to stop in.
